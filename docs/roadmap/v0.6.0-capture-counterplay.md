# CdrBounty v0.6.0 — Capture & Target Counterplay

**Status:** Planned

## Goal

Make the target an active participant in bounty gameplay. This phase adds alive contracts, capture/delivery flow, jail/bail, evasion tools, and meaningful consequences for defeating hunters.

## Contract Resolution Modes

Planned modes:

- `DEAD` — normal lethal claim.
- `ALIVE` — target must be captured and delivered.
- `DEAD_OR_ALIVE` — contract may be completed through either configured resolution path with separate reward rules.

Resolution mode is part of the contract definition and must not be inferred solely from the final damage event.

## Capture State Machine

Suggested states:

- `FREE`
- `CAPTURE_ELIGIBLE`
- `RESTRAINED`
- `IN_TRANSPORT`
- `DELIVERED`
- `ESCAPED`
- `RELEASED`

Capture must be a state-machine feature rather than a permanent potion-effect hack.

## Capture Trigger

Potential configurable triggers:

- Target reaches configured health threshold.
- Hunter uses designated capture item/action.
- Target is combat-tagged and meets alive-contract conditions.

Safety requirements:

- No capture in disabled worlds/regions.
- Respect invulnerability/spectator/admin bypass rules.
- Prevent repeated capture event spam.
- Capture state clears safely on disconnect/restart based on configured policy.

## Transport & Delivery

Alive contracts may require the hunter to deliver a captured target to:

- Configured coordinates/region.
- Citizens bounty authority NPC when integration is enabled.
- Command-defined authority endpoint.

Transport rules may configure:

- Maximum transport time.
- Maximum distance from captor.
- Damage policy.
- Teleport restrictions.
- Disconnect handling.
- Captor death behavior.

## Jail & Bail

After successful alive delivery:

- Target may enter a temporary sentence.
- Sentence duration is contract/config driven.
- Bail may be enabled.
- Bail can split value among configured destinations such as hunter, system sink, and issuer.

Bail settlement must use the same transaction-safety principles as bounty rewards.

## Target Counterplay

### Signal Scrambler

Temporarily weakens or disables Hunter Compass signal for eligible hunters.

Configurable properties:

- Duration.
- Cooldown.
- Cost/item requirement.
- Maximum uses per contract/window.
- Intel level needed to resist disruption.

### False Trail / Decoy

Creates stale or false tracking information without modifying the target's true server location.

Requirements:

- Clearly bounded lifetime.
- Cannot leak another unrelated player's actual location.
- Debug tooling identifies whether an intel result is real, stale, or decoy-generated.

### Escape

Target may escape capture due to:

- Distance break.
- Captor death.
- Timeout.
- Configured interaction/task.
- Administrative recovery.

Escape produces a deterministic contract consequence and audit entry.

## Hunter Killer Consequences

If a target kills an eligible hunter during an active hunt, configurable effects may include:

- Heat increase.
- Bounty escalation.
- Survival streak increment.
- Temporary Intel reduction for that hunter.
- Target achievement/stat tracking.

This must only trigger for legitimate hunter-target relationships.

## Revenge Contracts

After a valid player death, the victim may be offered a simplified revenge-contract creation flow.

Safeguards:

- Standard escrow rules still apply.
- Cooldown.
- Minimum/maximum amount.
- Anti-collusion protections.
- Revenge flow cannot bypass contract creation permissions.

## Permissions

Planned additions:

- `cdrbounty.capture.use`
- `cdrbounty.capture.transport`
- `cdrbounty.counterplay.scrambler`
- `cdrbounty.counterplay.decoy`
- `cdrbounty.revenge.create`
- `cdrbounty.admin.capture`
- `cdrbounty.bypass.capture`

## Acceptance Criteria

v0.6.0 is complete when:

- DEAD, ALIVE, and DEAD_OR_ALIVE contracts resolve using correct payout paths.
- Capture state cannot be duplicated or remain permanently stuck after normal failure cases.
- Captor death/disconnect and target disconnect/restart policies behave as configured.
- Delivery validates the correct target, contract, and hunter.
- Jail sentence and bail survive restart where applicable.
- Scramblers and false trails affect only authorized tracking data.
- Escape paths produce one deterministic result.
- Revenge contracts use normal escrow and anti-abuse rules.
- Admin recovery tools can safely release or resolve stuck capture states.

## Explicitly Out of Scope

- Multi-hunter teams.
- Bounty chains.
- Cross-server transport/capture.
- Built-in custom NPC engine.
