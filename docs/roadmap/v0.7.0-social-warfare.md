# CdrBounty v0.7.0 — Social Warfare & Dynamic Hunts

**Status:** Planned

## Goal

Create emergent multiplayer conflicts around the bounty ecosystem: hunter teams, rivalries, bounty chains, shared payouts, and dynamic server-wide hunt events.

## Hunter Teams

Teams may accept compatible contracts together.

Core rules:

- Team owner/leader.
- Member list.
- Maximum size.
- Join/leave policy.
- Shared contract reservations.
- Payout split strategy.
- Contribution tracking.
- Team disband handling.

Possible payout modes:

- Equal split.
- Damage/contribution weighted.
- Leader-defined percentages subject to server policy.
- First-completion-only.

Payout logic must be deterministic and auditable.

## Rival Hunter Rules

When multiple hunters pursue the same target, servers may configure:

- Hunter-vs-hunter PvP enabled/disabled.
- Interference penalties.
- Last-hit versus participation rules.
- Exclusive-contract protection.
- Assist rewards.

The plugin must not assume a single PvP philosophy; behavior should remain configurable.

## Bounty Chains

CdrBounty may detect directed bounty relationships such as:

`A -> B -> C -> A`

Chain features may include:

- Chain visualization.
- Combined chain value.
- Chain depth.
- Chain event announcements.
- Statistics for longest/highest-value active chains.

Chain detection must have strict depth/complexity limits to avoid expensive graph traversal on the main thread.

## Betrayal & Collusion Safeguards

Social systems expand abuse surface. Detection should consider:

- Same-team target kills.
- Immediate team leave before claim.
- Repeated hunter-target-team cycling.
- Payout redirection patterns.
- Shared-party/guild relationships when integrations expose them.

Automated denial should be configurable. Suspicious events should be recorded even when payout is allowed.

## Target Allies

Optional mechanics may record players assisting a wanted target, enabling:

- Assist statistics.
- Heat hooks.
- Hunter intel implications.
- Dynamic event objectives.

This system should not automatically punish normal nearby players without clear configured participation rules.

## Dynamic Hunt Events

CdrBounty may trigger server-wide events based on conditions such as:

- A Legendary Bounty appears.
- Public Enemy survives for a configured period.
- Bounty value crosses a threshold.
- A chain reaches configured size/value.

Event hooks may drive:

- Broadcasts.
- BossBars.
- Bonus reward multipliers.
- Temporary hunter slot expansion.
- Special objectives.

All automatic event rewards require caps and explicit settlement records.

## Seasonal Categories

Planned leaderboard categories expand to:

- Best Hunter.
- Most Wanted.
- Highest Single Claim.
- Longest Wanted Survival.
- Most Hunter Kills as Target.
- Most Successful Team.
- Most Perfect Hunts.
- Highest-Value Bounty Chain participation.

Season reset must archive historical results before clearing active season counters.

## Permissions

Planned additions:

- `cdrbounty.team.manage`
- `cdrbounty.chain.view`
- `cdrbounty.events.view`
- `cdrbounty.admin.team`
- `cdrbounty.admin.events`

## Acceptance Criteria

v0.7.0 is complete when:

- Team contract acceptance/resolution is deterministic.
- Payout splits sum exactly to the settled reward with defined rounding behavior.
- Leaving/disbanding teams cannot duplicate or orphan rewards.
- Exclusive contracts cannot be bypassed by social mechanics.
- Bounty chain detection is bounded and performant.
- Dynamic events cannot trigger recursively without configured safeguards.
- Seasonal snapshots preserve historical winners/statistics before reset.
- Suspicious team/collusion behavior is visible to admins.

## Explicitly Out of Scope

- Stable third-party developer API guarantees.
- Cross-server team synchronization.
- Redis/event-bus infrastructure.
- Web dashboards.
