# CdrBounty v0.4.0 — Intelligence & Tracking

**Status:** Planned

## Goal

Make target tracking a deliberate gameplay loop. Hunters should gather progressively better information instead of receiving unrestricted live coordinates.

## Intel Model

Each active hunt may maintain an Intel Confidence value, for example `0-100`.

Intel can reveal increasingly precise information:

- Online/offline status.
- Current dimension.
- Current biome.
- Last-seen world.
- Coarse distance band.
- Approximate radius.
- Short exact-location pulse.

Exact thresholds and costs must be configurable.

## Intel Sources

Initial sources may include:

- Passive time-based accumulation.
- Currency purchase.
- Hunter rank bonuses.
- Target activity triggers.
- Server-defined command/reward hooks.

Future integrations may provide additional Intel sources through provider APIs.

## Hunter Compass

The Hunter Compass should expose approximate guidance without becoming permanent wallhack behavior.

Planned signal bands:

- `NO_SIGNAL`
- `VERY_FAR`
- `FAR`
- `NEAR`
- `VERY_NEAR`
- `CRITICAL`

Rules:

- Only works for an accepted eligible contract.
- Respects disabled worlds/regions.
- Has refresh interval/cooldown.
- Does not constantly expose exact coordinates.
- Can be disrupted by target counterplay added in v0.6.0.

## Exact Location Pulse

High-level Intel may grant a temporary coordinate or marker pulse.

Requirements:

- Fixed short duration.
- Cooldown.
- Configurable cost.
- Clear timestamp so hunters know data may already be stale.
- No continuous async telelocation spam.

## Last Seen

Store a privacy-conscious last-seen snapshot for eligible bounty gameplay:

- World/dimension.
- Optional biome.
- Approximate location bucket if enabled.
- Timestamp.

Do not expose raw historical movement logs by default.

## Target Warning

Targets may receive warnings based on nearby eligible hunters:

- General feeling of being watched.
- Hunter nearby.
- Critical proximity.

Warning ranges and whether hunter identity is disclosed must be configurable.

## Intel Economy

Intel purchases should support:

- Vault cost.
- Cooldown.
- Per-contract purchase limits.
- Rank discounts/bonuses.
- Server tax/sink configuration.

Purchases require transaction records and must not deduct currency if the requested Intel cannot be delivered.

## World & Region Safety

Tracking must honor configured restrictions such as:

- Disabled worlds.
- Spawn/lobby worlds.
- WorldGuard regions when integration is present.
- Spectator/admin invisibility policy.
- Vanish integration hooks where available.

## Permissions

Planned additions:

- `cdrbounty.intel.use`
- `cdrbounty.intel.purchase`
- `cdrbounty.intel.compass`
- `cdrbounty.admin.intel.inspect`
- `cdrbounty.bypass.tracking`

## Acceptance Criteria

v0.4.0 is complete when:

- Intel confidence persists correctly.
- Information precision follows configured thresholds.
- Hunter Compass only tracks eligible accepted contracts.
- Exact-location pulses expire reliably.
- Intel purchases are atomic with economy transactions.
- Disabled worlds/regions never leak prohibited location data.
- Last-seen information clearly includes age/staleness.
- Tracking remains performant with many simultaneous contracts.
- Admin debug tooling can explain why a target is or is not trackable.

## Explicitly Out of Scope

- False trails and scramblers; these arrive with target counterplay in v0.6.0.
- Automatic Heat-generated targets.
- Cross-server location tracking.
- Web-map tracking.
